Pharmacy POS Management

Complete Pharmacy, Inventory, Prescription and POS Suite for Odoo 17

A scalable pharmacy ERP module for branches, medicines, batches, expiry alerts, stock transfers, purchasing, patient profiles, POS operations, accounting links, reports, dashboards and integration-ready APIs.

Compatible with Odoo Community and Enterprise.

Community Enterprise Odoo.sh Ready Arabic RTL

Key Highlights

Built with clean Odoo ORM models, access rights, branch rules, scheduled alerts, QWeb reports, REST endpoints and a POS-ready frontend layer.

Pharmacy Operations

Manage pharmacy profiles, companies, branches, branch users, managers, warehouses and cashboxes.

Medicine Control

Track barcode, QR, SKU, scientific names, dosage, UOM, product images, PDFs and alternatives.

Expiry & Stock

Batch management, FEFO helper logic, expired-sale blocking, near-expiry alerts and reorder thresholds.

Complete Feature Set

  • Multi-company and multi-branch pharmacy management
  • Branch cashboxes and user permissions per branch
  • Medicine, OTC, cosmetics, devices, supplements and baby products
  • Batch, lot, manufacturing date and expiry tracking
  • Inventory adjustments and branch stock transfers
  • Purchase RFQ, approvals, discounts, taxes and invoice links
  • POS orders with hold, refund and partial payments
  • Prescription upload, doctor details, refill and POS order links
  • Patient allergies, chronic diseases, insurance and credit management
  • Accounting journal, analytic account and cost center integration
  • Dashboards, pivot/graph views and QWeb PDF receipt
  • REST APIs for products, stock and reporting summaries

Security & Integration Ready

Includes pharmacy-specific groups for Administrator, Pharmacist, Cashier, Storekeeper and Accountant, with branch-aware record rules. Integration endpoint records are ready for WhatsApp, SMS, payment gateways, mobile applications, barcode devices and printers.
